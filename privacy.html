Privacy policy

ClipFactory · Effective 21 October 2026

ClipFactory is a private tool that its owner uses to cut short clips from long videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what Google and YouTube data ClipFactory uses and how.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, ClipFactory asks for permission to:

  • upload videos to the YouTube channel being connected, and set their title, description, tags and visibility;
  • read basic channel information, such as the channel name, to confirm which channel is connected.

ClipFactory does not access Gmail, contacts, Google Drive, viewing history or any other Google account data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access is used only to publish clips that the owner has approved.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to, shared with or sold to anyone.
  • ClipFactory has no user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

YouTube API Services

ClipFactory uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke ClipFactory's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working, and it can be deleted from the computer running ClipFactory. To ask for any data to be deleted, use the contact link below. Requests are handled within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
